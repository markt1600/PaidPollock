An algorithmic studio. Commission a Jackson Pollock pour — flung, dripped and pooled in enamel — a Joan Miró dream painted in ink, crayon and oil, or a Henri Matisse line drawing in a few confident strokes. All finished at archival resolution.

Paintings are thrown over about three seconds — physics-simulated pours under real gravity. Dream paintings are worked in ink, wax crayon and oil. Line drawings unfold stroke by stroke in pen, brush or crayon on wove paper. Everything renders at up to 4,400 px; downloads include your chosen frame. No two commissions repeat.
